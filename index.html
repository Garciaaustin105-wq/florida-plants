Every size also comes as a light version with a fraction of the polygons, the same shape and the same materials, for the middle and far distance of a scene.

FBX and glTF

For programs and game engines other than Blender, every version is baked to image textures (base colour, roughness, normal, leaf underside colour and translucency) and exported as FBX and glTF 2.0. Below, the glTF files imported into a fresh Blender, as a buyer would.

How they are made

The plants are built from botanical reference, real photos of each species, and its real structure: how the leaves grow, how a palm's fronds sit, how a hedge is clipped. The 3D models and materials are generated by Blender Python scripts written by an AI coding assistant under my direction, then reviewed and corrected by hand against the reference photos, plant by plant. No text-to-3D, image-to-3D or AI image generators were used.
